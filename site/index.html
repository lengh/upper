Made for reading and replying, fast

Every choice is borrowed from two decades of IRC clients — WeeChat, irssi, catgirl, senpai — and adapted to Discord.

│

A timeline you can scan

Names right-aligned against a rule, hanging indents, times only when they change, a red new line where you stopped reading.

1:

The hotlist

What's waiting sits in the status bar, numbered. Alt+A jumps to the next, Alt+1…9 to any of them.

❯

A composer that helps

Tab-completes @names, #channels and :emoji:. s/old/new fixes typos, +:fire: reacts. Drafts survive restarts.

⌘

Jump anywhere

Ctrl+K fuzzy-finds any channel or DM and shows why it matched. Alt+/ flips back.

●

Respects your attention

Marks things read only while you're looking. Mentions ring the bell and show in your tab title. Muted servers stay quiet.

⚡

Light and resilient

One static binary. Compressed gateway, resumes after drops without reloading, stays within Discord's rate limits by design.

Keys worth knowing

Press F1 inside upper for all of them.

Ctrl+Kjump to any channel or DM
Alt+Anext conversation with activity
Alt+/back to the previous channel
Tabcomplete names, channels, emoji
↑edit your last message
Ctrl+Jnew line
Ctrl+↑select a message: reply, react, copy…
Alt+Uscroll to the first unread

Install

The one-liner downloads the right build, verifies its SHA-256 checksum, installs to ~/.local/bin and puts it on your PATH. Run it again to update.

# Ubuntu on WSL (or any Linux, x86-64 or ARM64)
curl -fsSL https://lengh.github.io/upper/install.sh | sh

# prefer to read it first?
curl -fsSLO https://lengh.github.io/upper/install.sh
less install.sh && sh install.sh

Or download a build yourself — version {{VERSION}}, built {{DATE}}:

PlatformFileSHA-256
Linux / WSL x86-64upper_linux_amd64.tar.gz{{SHA_AMD64}}
Linux / WSL ARM64upper_linux_arm64.tar.gz{{SHA_ARM64}}
 
# manual install
tar -xzf upper_linux_amd64.tar.gz
install -m 755 upper ~/.local/bin/upper

Use Windows Terminal for true colour, mouse support and emoji. Uninstall with rm ~/.local/bin/upper; upper --logout forgets your token first.

Before you log in: upper uses your personal account token. Discord's Terms of Service don't allow third-party clients, and accounts have occasionally been flagged for using them. upper keeps the risk low — it identifies itself honestly, never scrapes or automates, and stays far inside rate limits — but it isn't zero. Your token is stored only on your machine, readable only by you.
